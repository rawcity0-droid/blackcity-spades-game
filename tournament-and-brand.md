## 2. Tournament format
**Entry**
- Free for players to enter. No money in, no money out for players (avoids gambling/sweepstakes issues). Hosting your own tournament is part of the paid plan (section 5).
- Open to anyone, anywhere. Nickname only, no account.
- Join with a code. Solo joiners get matched with another solo or a bot partner. Empty team slots are filled by bot teams at start.
- Start rule: the organizer starts it, or it auto-starts at the set time.
- Who can host: subscribers (Host plan). Anyone can play in a tournament.

**Bracket**
- Single elimination, 4, 8 or 16 teams. Seeding is random.
- Winners advance automatically. Bot-vs-bot matches are simulated instantly.
- Each match is one game of partnership Spades.

**Scoring**
- Standard partnership scoring: 10 points per bid trick, 1 per extra trick (bag), 10 bags = -100, nil +100/-100.
- **Game to 500** by default. Recommendation to cut usage and keep rounds moving: games to **250 in early rounds, 500 in the final**. Raw/CEO decide; PLAN.md currently says 250 for all matches.
- A team at -200 loses immediately.

**Tie rules**
- Both teams reach the target in the same hand: higher score wins.
- Exactly equal scores: play one extra hand; the higher score after it wins. Repeat until one team is ahead.

**Dropped players**
- A bot takes over a disconnected seat right away. The player can retake the seat at the start of the next hand using the reconnect token.
- If a whole team leaves, the other team advances.

**Prizes**
- Bragging rights only for v1: winner and runner-up shown on the bracket page and a "Champions" line on the home page. No cash, no gift cards.
- Later option: a shareable champion badge.

## 7. Card-art brief (for Raw Assist 1)
**Goal:** big, colorful, readable cards with original art. Do not copy any existing deck (no Bicycle-style layout or artwork, no existing characters or logos). Cards are drawn in SVG/CSS so no licensed art.

**Readability rules (most important)**
- Rank and suit in large type in two opposite corners; readable on a phone at about 60 px wide.
- Pip cards (A–10) use big, simple pips; the ace is one very large pip.
- High contrast: dark outline around figures and pips, plain light card face (not busy).
- Suit never relies on color alone: shapes are distinct, and spades carry a thick outline so they read at a glance.
- Rank labels: A, 2–10, J, Q, K (no tiny text).

**Suit styling (colorful, four distinct colors)**
- Spades: deep indigo/black-violet, with a gold outline. The trump suit, so it looks special.
- Hearts: hot coral-red.
- Diamonds: bright orange or amber. Use a distinct color from hearts so they aren't confused.
- Clubs: emerald green.
- Each suit has its own accent corner shape and background tint on the face cards so the suit shows across the table.
- Card back: bold Blackcity pattern, a single spade in the center, rich color.

**Character concepts (original, 12 face cards, 4 suits × J/Q/K)**
Themed on city life: "Blackcity" neighborhood characters. All friendly, bold-shaped, flat-color with thick outlines. Faces large and expressive, no real people's likeness.
- **Jacks — "the Hustlers":** young, energetic, in motion. Spades: skateboarding courier in a violet hoodie. Hearts: street-food vendor holding a heart-shaped pie. Diamonds: bike-messenger with an amber helmet. Clubs: park gardener with a watering can and a green cap.
- **Queens — "the Bosses":** confident, stylish, regal. Spades: a jazz singer in a violet gown with a gold microphone. Hearts: a bakery owner in a coral apron with a crown of pastry. Diamonds: a fashion designer with an amber scarf and scissors. Clubs: a community garden queen with a leaf crown.
- **Kings — "the Legends":** older, strong, warm. Spades: a drum-line captain with a gold drum-major hat. Hearts: a barbecue king in a coral chef coat. Diamonds: a vintage car restorer with amber goggles. Clubs: a coach with a green varsity jacket.
- Each court figure is half-body, mirrored top and bottom like standard cards, with the suit symbol held or worn so it reads at a glance.

**Deliverables:** one SVG per card (52) plus a back, or a generated set from a shared template. Raw Assist 1 can ship the pips and court cards first, then refine the characters.
